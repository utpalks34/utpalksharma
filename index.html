Who I am

I'm Utpal Kant Sharma, a final-year B.Tech Computer Science student at The Assam Kaziranga University (CGPA 8.08/10), based in Guwahati, Assam. My work sits where language models meet real backend engineering: agent orchestration, retrieval systems and the APIs and data layers that keep them honest. I learn by shipping — every project below started as a question I couldn't answer from documentation alone.

What I do

I design agentic AI systems with LangChain and LangGraph — planner/retriever/responder graphs, guardrails, vector search and evaluation suites — and build the backends around them in Django REST Framework and FastAPI with PostgreSQL, PostGIS and JWT auth. Alongside that: SQL performance work, ML pipelines in Python, and deployment with Docker on AWS.
